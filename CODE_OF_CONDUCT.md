# Code of Conduct

This project is run by and for students and mentors. We want it to be a good place to learn.

* Be kind and respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
* Be patient with beginners and with people whose first language is not yours.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or sexual or violent content, in issues, pull
  requests, discussions, or anywhere you represent the project.
* Keep feedback specific and constructive; assume good intent.
* Do not share other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments and contributions that break these rules, and may
block repeat offenders.

## Reporting

If you experience or see a problem, report it privately through GitHub (repository **Security** tab,
*Report a vulnerability*, and say it is a conduct report) or by contacting a maintainer listed in the
repository. Reports are kept confidential.
